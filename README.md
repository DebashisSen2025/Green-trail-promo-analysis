## Green-trail-promo-analysis

Data-driven analysis of GreenTrail Outdoors’ promotional strategies using sales, visits, and retention metrics.  
Includes recommendations for control groups, test duration, and sustainability-aligned marketing approaches.  

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This repository contains my consulting-style analysis of GreenTrail Outdoors’ past promotions.  
The goal is to identify which strategies drive sustainable customer engagement and propose optimized promotional tactics.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights
- **Urban stores** outperform suburban stores by 48% in average sales lift.  
- **Special Events** deliver the strongest post-promotion retention (24.5%).  
- **Discounts** boost short-term sales but create price dependency.  
- **BOGO offers** show negative retention, indicating stockpiling behavior.  

---

## 🧩 Methodology
- Analyzed weekly sales, visits, and retention across 25 stores.  
- Applied matched-pair control design (store type, size, baseline sales, region).  
- Recommended **10–12 week test duration** to measure true behavioral change.  

---

## 🌱 Recommendations
1. **Experiential Events** – sustainability workshops, gear repair clinics, outdoor skills training.  
2. **Sustainability Rewards Program** – points for eco-behaviors like recycling and reusable bags.  
3. **Trade-In & Upcycle Initiatives** – refurbish and resell gear to reduce waste and attract conscious consumers.  

---

## 📌 Next Steps

- Launch pilot tests with matched control groups.
